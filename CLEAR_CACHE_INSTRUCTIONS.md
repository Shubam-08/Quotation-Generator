# Clear Browser Cache - Step by Step

## The changes ARE in the code, but your browser is caching the old JavaScript.

## Follow these steps EXACTLY:

### Step 1: Close ALL browser tabs with your app

### Step 2: Clear browser cache (Choose your browser):

#### **Chrome / Edge:**
1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
2. Select "Cached images and files"
3. Time range: "Last hour" or "All time"
4. Click "Clear data"

#### **Firefox:**
1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
2. Select "Cache"
3. Time range: "Last hour"
4. Click "Clear Now"

### Step 3: Close browser completely

### Step 4: Reopen browser and go to your app

### Step 5: When the page loads, do a HARD REFRESH:
- Press `Ctrl + F5` OR
- Press `Ctrl + Shift + R`

### Step 6: Test the PDF export
